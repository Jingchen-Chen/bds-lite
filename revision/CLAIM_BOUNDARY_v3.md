## Interpretation rules

- C01: On the studied datasets and under the prespecified estimator, the in-network distillation-plus-gate addition did not establish a Boundary F1 gain over the boundary-decoder reference. An interval crossing zero does not establish equivalence. The transfer comparison without surface loss is +0.0092 [−0.0010, +0.0194] pooled; three runs per cell and dataset. `revision/tables/families/expansion_interaction.csv`.
- C02: In the ISIC2018 retrained set, the full BDS-Lite recipe scored below the decoder-only reference in Boundary F1: ten-run difference −0.0321 [−0.0518, −0.0124], fresh-seed Holm p=0.0314. It contains multiple components; do not attribute this deficit to distillation or extend it to the earlier pipeline, where the difference was not established. `revision/tables/families/expansion_contrasts.csv`; `revision/tables/families/historical_april.csv`.
- C03: Exploratory external-teacher band KD versus decoder-only had positive Boundary F1 point estimates on ISIC2018, ACDC and Synapse; none survived Holm correction across the three datasets (0.496, 0.088, 0.500). ISIC2018 uses 3 KD and 10 decoder runs; ACDC and Synapse use 3 per arm. These do not establish transfer. `revision/kd_unified/contrasts.csv`.
- C04: In the pre-registered replication on 50 previously unused official ACDC test patients, 17 fresh seeds per arm, band KD minus decoder-only was +0.0178 [95% CI +0.0098, +0.0258], p=0.0000784. The decision was success **of unclear size**: the lower bound is below the pre-registered 0.01 smallest effect of interest. ACDC was selected after exploratory results; the result is limited to these patients and this protocol. The original design did not isolate the band. `preregistration/acdc_confirmation.md`; `revision/acdc_confirm/results/results.json`.
- C05: A full-image KD control **now exists**, contrary to the time-specific v2 rationale. It and the compact-teacher control were registered before their own training but after the ACDC test results were examined, and reused those patients and the earlier reference runs. They are secondary and exploratory, not a new independent confirmation. Both 17-run controls have higher Boundary F1 than decoder-only in the registered four-comparison Holm family: full KD +0.0106 [+0.0034, +0.0177], Holm p=0.0196; compact-teacher band KD +0.0118 [+0.0037, +0.0200], Holm p=0.0196. `revision/followup/results/contrasts.csv`; `scripts/kd_followup/launch.py`; `scripts/kd_mc/loss.py`.
- C06: The incremental effects of the band and of the pretrained larger teacher are **unresolved** in that family. Band minus full is +0.0073 [−0.0005, +0.0150], Holm p=0.0945; external-teacher band minus compact-teacher band is +0.0060 [+0.0001, +0.0119], unadjusted p=0.0473 but Holm p=0.0945. The latter nominal 95% interval excludes zero but the multiplicity-adjusted test does not. Neither establishes equivalence, absence, or necessity. Teacher pretraining and capacity remain confounded; each teacher was trained once. Loss normalization and gradient scale also differ between full and band regions. `revision/followup/results/contrasts.csv`; `revision/followup_registration.md`.
- C07: Secondary and per-structure results are descriptive and cannot replace the stage-3 primary decision. HD95/ASSD are computed on the resized 2D grid, in pixels, with undefined distances omitted in the primary analysis. Do not assert physical-distance or 3D performance.
- C08: The double-counted run-by-unit residual and post hoc sensitivity estimates must be disclosed. Their intervals can change sign and are not the prespecified decision procedure; truncation and reweighting produce non-monotonic sensitivity. They do not alter C01 or C04. `revision/CLAIM_BOUNDARY_v2_NOTE_20261109.md`; `revision/restructure_posthoc/`; `revision/followup/results/posthoc_reference_estimators.csv`.
- C09: Feature, gradient, gate, contour and error-location probes are diagnostic observations or hypotheses. They do not establish a causal mechanism. No direct contrast supports a difference-of-effects claim between in-network transfer and the external-teacher confirmation, which use different patients, runs and trainers.
- C10: Earlier ISIC2018 validation numbers affected by training-image overlap are withdrawn; Experiment C is incomplete and its test outcomes are excluded under its existing blinding boundary. E3 augmentation is ongoing and outside this cutoff; no preliminary metric is interpreted.
- C11: Teacher-dependent training cost is separate from deployed-graph cost. The auxiliary boundary decoder and external teacher are training-only; the BDS-Lite gate is present at inference unless made identity or collapsed. One must distinguish exact structural identity from experimentally measured prediction or latency differences.
- C12: Published scores and adapted implementations are context under different protocols, not matched head-to-head trials. Do not infer statistical rankings from cross-paper numbers. Keep negative and inconclusive results; do not claim performance beyond the studied 2D datasets or conditions.
